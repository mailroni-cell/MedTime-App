Medication reminders out loud, full screen, big and simple
```
**Description:**
```
MedTime is a medication reminder app that is simple and clear for every age.

At the time you set, the phone rings, shows the medicine name and dose on the full screen and reads them out loud. One tap marks that you took it.

What you get:

• The reminder read out loud, in 6 languages

• First aid and nearby pharmacies
